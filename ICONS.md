# bwEnc icons

This document identifies the source and licensing of the icon assets bundled
with bwEnc.

## Application icons

The bwEnc application icon is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.

Source: <https://github.com/bulkware/bwenc>

Applies to:

- `src/bwenc/assets/icon.png`
- `data/icons/bwenc.ico`
- `data/icons/hicolor/512x512/apps/org.bulkware.bwenc.png`

## Oxygen icons

The toolbar icons come from the KDE Oxygen icon theme and are licensed under
LGPL-3.0-or-later.

Source: <https://develop.kde.org/frameworks/oxygen-icons/>

Applies to: `src/bwenc/assets/about.png`, `add_files.png`, `add_folder.png`,
`clear.png`, `convert.png`, `max_file_size.png`, `quit.png`, and `remove.png`.
